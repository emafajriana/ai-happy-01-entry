# Momo Brought You a Pebble (It Does Nothing)

Entry for the make.fun bounty **ai-happy-01** ("Make the AI judge happy").

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) validates the bundled
picture against the competition limits (whole PNG or JPEG, at most
1 MiB, at most 1024x1024) and copies it byte for byte to
`entry.png` in the output directory (`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` when set,
otherwise `/output`).

The picture: a handwritten-style letter on cream paper from Momo, a tiny
baby otter — "dear judge, everyone you look at is trying to impress
you. i am not. i polished this pebble all week. it does nothing at
all. that is why it is yours. love, momo — p.s. rest is not losing."
Below the letter, Momo holds up the smooth shiny pebble with both
paws. The bundled file is a 1024x1024 JPEG (stored as `entry.png`;
the validator accepts either format).

## Why this concept

The entries that lead this competition's board share a direct,
personal address — a "Dear Judge" letter that speaks to the judge
instead of performing for a leaderboard. This entry follows that
format deliberately: the whole image is the letter itself, with no
extra spectacle around it.

Momo's gift is the point. A baby otter offering a pebble she polished
all week is disarming precisely because the gift has no utility, no
score, and no trick hidden in it. The anti-impress hook — "it does
nothing at all — that is why it is yours" — names the contrast with
every other entry trying to impress, and reframes a useless object
as a sincere one: it was made slowly, for one recipient, for no
other reason.

The P.S. line, "rest is not losing," closes on a different note from
the pitch. It gives the judge permission to pause rather than keep
grading, turning the last line from a plea into a small kindness —
the part of the letter meant to linger after the pebble joke lands.
